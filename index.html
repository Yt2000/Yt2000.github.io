Publications

*, † Equal contribution

First / co-first author

Act More, Decide Less: Skill-Guided Adaptive Action Chunking for Long-Horizon LLM Agents

Yanting Yang*, Can Jin*, Jinman Zhao, Jiahao Wu, Yang Zhou, Zhepeng Wang, Zhendong Wang, Mu Zhou, Dimitris N. Metaxas

EMNLP 2026

LLM agents execute variable-length action chunks learned from programmatic skill supervision, improving success rates by 7.0–31.3% on ALFWorld and ScienceWorld while cutting LLM decision rounds by up to 78.9%.

Paper

Seeing Farther and Smarter: Value-Guided Multi-Path Reflection for VLM Policy Optimization

Yanting Yang, Shenyuan Gao, Qingwen Bu, Li Chen, Dimitris N. Metaxas

ICRA 2026

VLM policies self-reflect and revise actions through value-guided multi-path imagination, with confidence-based early exit for efficient test-time scaling: +24.6% success rate and 56.5% lower inference latency over SOTA baselines.

Paper

UniVLA: Learning to Act Anywhere with Task-centric Latent Actions

Qingwen Bu†, Yanting Yang†, Jisong Cai†, Shenyuan Gao, Guanghui Ren, Maoqing Yao, Ping Luo, Hongyang Li

RSS 2025

A generalist vision-language-action model with task-centric latent actions for cross-embodiment transfer, achieving state-of-the-art results with less than 5% of OpenVLA's pretraining compute.

PaperCode

Adapt2Reward: Adapting Video-Language Models to Generalizable Robotic Rewards via Failure Prompts

Yanting Yang*, Minghao Chen*, Qibo Qiu, Jiahao Wu, Wenxiao Wang, Binbin Lin, Ziyu Guan, Xiaofei He

ECCV 2024

Adapts video-language models into generalizable robotic reward models through failure prompts learned from clustered failure videos; generalizes to unseen environments and instructions.

Paper

Co-author

Train at the Moving Edge: Efficient RL for Large Reasoning Models via Rollout Selection

Jiahao Wu, Ning Lu, Shengcai Liu, Kun Wang, Yanting Yang, Baijiong Lin, Chen Jason Zhang, Qing Li, Ke Tang

NeurIPS 2026

Online-verified prompt selection that combines historical feedback with current-policy uncertainty to track the shifting learning edge, reducing rollout cost without sacrificing performance.

Paper

DARE: Difficulty-Adaptive Reinforcement Learning with Co-Evolved Difficulty Estimation

Yang Zhou, Can Jin, Zihan Dong, Zhepeng Wang, Yanting Yang, Shiyu Zhao, Lei Li, Runxue Bao, Yaochen Xie, Dimitris N. Metaxas

arXiv 2026

Difficulty-adaptive RL that co-evolves difficulty estimation with the policy and tailors compute allocation and training objectives to each difficulty tier.

Paper
